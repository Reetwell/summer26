BreakfastProtein pancakes

How to make them
1. Blend oats into flour using a blender or NutriBullet
2. Mix oat flour + whey + eggs + milk until smooth batter
3. Heat a non-stick pan on medium, add tiny spray of oil
4. Pour small circles (~8cm), cook 2 mins each side until golden
5. Top with Greek yogurt + berries + drizzle of honey
Makes ~6 small pancakes
