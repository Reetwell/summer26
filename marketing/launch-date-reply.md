# Reply to marketing — App Store launch date

**Announce: late August 2026 — countdown to Friday 29 August 2026.** That's the date I'd
stake the campaign on (~80% confidence). Keep the creative flexible enough to absorb a
±1 week slip.

There's an earlier internal target (~4 Aug) the build team is aiming at, but **do not
announce it** — it depends on three things going perfectly that are outside our control.
Announcing it and missing it hurts more than launching early quietly.

**One hard rule for all assets:** do **not** print or lock the app **name** yet. The name
isn't trademark-cleared, and it's the single biggest schedule risk — a forced rename would
invalidate finished artwork. Use `[NAME]` / `[LAUNCH DATE]` placeholders in mockups until
we confirm both.

**Why 29 Aug and not sooner** — three non-engineering risks set the buffer: (1) app name +
trademark clearance (the long pole), (2) a possible data-sync bug under investigation,
(3) Apple's review of a health app aimed at minors (budget one rejection cycle). The
engineering itself is ~2 weeks; the buffer covers those three.

**What moves the date:** if the name clears and the sync bug is shallow by mid-July, we
hold or beat 29 Aug. If the name needs changing, it slips to September — I'll tell you the
day we know.

**Go/no-go:** I'll send a confirmation by **~15 August** once the app is submitted to Apple.
