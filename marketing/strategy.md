# BB — Marketing Strategy (v1)

> **App name is NOT final.** "Build Your Body" (ex-"SummerBody") is a working title. See [aso.md](aso.md) for the name recommendation. Throughout this doc, "BB" = the app.
>
> **Launch markets:** UK + US. **Content engine:** faceless / screen-first (no talent required). **Acquisition model:** organic + creator/UGC-led. Paid *targeting* to under-18s is effectively unavailable (see §4).

---

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** *The gym app that doesn't feel like a spreadsheet — free, and it learns from the TikToks you already save.*

**Who it's for:** 14–21 y/o who are into training and food via TikTok, but are priced out of and bored by the serious adult apps (MyFitnessPal, Whoop). They want something that feels like the apps they already use and is genuinely good on the free tier.

**What we stand for (and against):**

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Effort-based — rank/league rewards showing up, not weight | Weight-loss / "shredded by summer" |
| Non-shaming, body-neutral | Before/after, calorie-guilt, "fix your body" |
| Free feels premium | Paywalled to be useful |
| Fits your life (learns from your TikToks) | A spreadsheet you have to feed |
| "Stay Focused" — consistency & skill | Hustle-culture / punishment |

**Why the anti-column matters:** the audience are minors. Body-image and weight-loss angles are policy-limited for teens on every major platform and under UK ad rules (see §4). Our brand ethos (effort-based ranks) and the compliance reality point the *same direction* — so this isn't a compromise, it's the wedge.

**Category framing:** don't compete as "another fitness tracker." Frame as **"your training + food, on autopilot, free"** — the three magic moments are (1) paste a TikTok → get a saved recipe, (2) meal plan → auto shopping list, (3) show up → rank up.

## 2. Channel plan (TikTok-first)

**Priority order:** TikTok → Instagram Reels → YouTube Shorts. Same vertical master, re-cut per platform.

| Channel | Role | Cadence (first 90 days) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Primary. Discovery engine, trend-native, where the audience already learns training/food | 1 post/day min, push to 2 | Owned account + creator posts. Screen-first content native here. |
| **IG Reels** | Secondary. Repurpose TikToks; slightly older skew (18–21) | 5–7/week | Reels + carousel "recipe from a TikTok" saves. |
| **YT Shorts** | Tertiary. Long-tail search ("how to meal prep as a student") | 3–5/week | Shorts double as ASO preview-video source. |
| **App Store / Play listing** | Conversion. Everything above funnels here | — | See [aso.md](aso.md). |

**Not now (with reason):**
- **Paid social** — targeting under-18s is blocked/near-useless (§4). Reconsider only for an 18–21-only campaign later.
- **Founder-on-camera / talking head** — you chose faceless/screen-first. Kept out of the engine; optional later.
- **X / Facebook / Snap** — wrong audience or wrong format for v1. Snap could return for hyper-local student pushes.

## 3. Content pillars (what we post)

Every video maps to a **real feature** (no vaporware). Five pillars, all buildable from screen recordings + text-on-motion + trending audio:

1. **"Paste a TikTok → recipe"** (the hero party trick). Show the import flow end to end. Highest share/save potential; it's a *demo*, not a claim.
2. **"Auto shopping list"** — meal plan → categorised list → Copy / Send-to-Reminders. Utility content, screenshots-to-Reminders.
3. **"Rank up by showing up"** — effort-based rank/league. Non-shaming progress; leaderboard/streak dopamine.
4. **"Today-forward training"** — open app, today's session hero, muscle map, animated demo, mark done. "POV: the app just tells you what to do today."
5. **"Readiness check-in"** — 10-second check-in tunes today's session. "Tired? The app already adjusted."

**Format rotation (screen-first toolkit):** screen-recording demos · text-on-motion "3 things this free app does" · trend/audio hijacks · green-screen-over-UI reactions (creator layer) · "apps on my phone" listicles · POV captions. Keep the green `#00694c` / cream `#F9F6F0` and DM Serif look consistent so clips are recognisable at a glance.

**Ratio:** ~60% utility/demo (saves + sends), ~25% relatable/POV (comments + shares), ~15% behind-the-build / community (trust). Optimise early posts for **saves and shares**, not likes — saves signal utility and feed reach for how-to content.

## 4. The paid-vs-organic reality for a minors audience (READ THIS)

**Bottom line: you cannot meaningfully run paid *acquisition* aimed at 14–17 year-olds. Plan for organic + creators, full stop.** Details, so nobody wastes budget:

**TikTok Ads**
- No **personalized** ads to 13–17 in the UK/EEA/Switzerland (and BR/MX/CO) at all.
- In the US, teen targeting is stripped back to **broad signals only** — location, language, device. No interests, no behaviours, no custom audiences, no gender.
- Ads/landing pages **must not feature minors** in at-risk content; age-restricted verticals auto-rejected.

**Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Ads**
- Teen (under-18) targeting is limited to **age + location** (gender was removed in 2023; the April 2026 rollout keeps it to age/location/gender-locked defaults). No interest/behaviour targeting; on-platform engagement won't inform their ads; geo no finer than city/state.

**What that means in practice:** even if you pay, you can't reach *the right* under-18s with any precision, and you can't lean on the body/fitness *interest* signals that would make a fitness ad efficient. Spend would leak to the wrong people. **So:** budget goes to **creators and content production**, not ad auctions.

**What paid IS allowed for (later, optional):**
- Campaigns explicitly **18–21 only** (exclude under-18), where normal targeting returns — useful for a post-launch scale push to the older half of the audience.
- **Boosting/whitelisting** high-performing organic creator posts to an 18+ audience.

**UK ad-content rules that bind us even organically (ASA/CAP):**
- **No weight-loss / body-image angles** that suggest a normal body needs "fixing" — ruled irresponsible repeatedly; and cosmetic-intervention ads are banned from targeting under-18s. We avoid the whole territory by design.
- **Clear #ad disclosure** on every paid creator post — must be prominent, not hidden behind "…more." (Full rules in [creator-ugc-brief.md](creator-ugc-brief.md).)

**UK Age-Appropriate Design Code (AADC) — marketing implications:**
- Data minimisation + high-privacy defaults are a *product* obligation, but marketing must not imply profiling of minors or push dark-pattern urgency. Keep CTAs honest ("free to start"), no fake scarcity, no data-hungry lead-gen forms aimed at minors.
- **Pre-launch dependency (product lane, not ours):** published **privacy policy** + **data-deletion flow** + age gate must exist before we drive install volume. Flagged so marketing doesn't outrun compliance. *(Tracked in the project note's Open questions.)*

## 5. Launch sequence (first ~90 days)

**Phase 0 — Foundation (pre-launch, ~2 wks)**
- Lock working name + handles (see [aso.md](aso.md)); secure @handle on TikTok/IG/YT.
- Set up owned TikTok; post 5–8 "building this" + demo clips to warm the account (algorithms favour accounts with history before you scale).
- Finalise ASO listing + preview video.
- Recruit first 5–10 micro-creators ([creator-ugc-brief.md](creator-ugc-brief.md)).
- **Gate check:** privacy policy + deletion flow + age gate live? If not, hold the paid-creator push.

**Phase 1 — Seed (wks 1–4)**
- Owned account: 1–2 posts/day, lead with the **TikTok→recipe** party trick.
- First creator wave posts; you repost/duet the best.
- Goal: find 1–2 formats that hit saves/shares. Double down, don't diversify.

**Phase 2 — Amplify (wks 5–8)**
- Scale the winning format; expand creator roster to ~20.
- Start an 18–21-only boost test on the single best organic clip.
- Launch a lightweight referral/streak-share loop if product supports it (idea → Inbox if not built).

**Phase 3 — Compound (wks 9–12)**
- UGC flywheel: templatise the winning hooks so any creator can reproduce.
- Begin YT Shorts search plays ("student meal prep", "gym plan for beginners").
- Review: which pillar drove installs? Re-weight pillars for the next quarter.

**North-star metric:** installs from organic + creators. **Leading indicators:** save rate and share rate per post (utility signal) → these predict reach before installs move.

## 6. Risks & open questions (marketing lane)

- **Name not final** — every asset here is find-and-replace-able; don't print merch/hard assets until locked + trademark-checked. See [aso.md](aso.md).
- **Recipe-import reliability** is the hero hook but relies on fragile oEmbed/scraping — if a demo video shows it failing, that's a bad look. Only publish import demos against links known to work; never promise "any link."
- **Compliance gate** (privacy policy / deletion / age gate) is a *product* dependency that blocks the paid-creator push — not ours to build, but ours to respect.
- **Free-tier promise** — all messaging leans "free." If monetisation later paywalls a magic moment, messaging must be revisited to stay honest (AADC + trust).
