# BB — 60s Hero Ad (TikTok / Reels, voiceover-led)

> **Placement:** TikTok / Instagram Reels, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920. **Carried by:** voiceover (AI voice or a young-sounding creator read) + on-screen text + music. **Faceless / screen-first** — every visual is a screen recording of the app.
> **Structure:** hook-led problem→solution tour (earn the first 3s with the party trick, one problem stab, tour the magic moments, close on the non-shaming ethos + CTA).
> **Working name only** — the app name isn't final ([aso.md](aso.md)). Uses `[NAME]` as a placeholder; swap once locked.
> **Guardrail:** no weight-loss/body-image framing; only demo the TikTok-import on a link known to import cleanly.

**Runtime:** 60s · **VO length:** ~155 words (natural TikTok pace ≈ 2.6 wps). **Muted-first:** on-screen text must carry the whole story with sound off.

## Coming-soon variant (pre-launch)

Until the app is live, run this as a **"Coming Soon"** ad. Everything above stays the same **except the ending**. Full plan: [coming-soon-campaign.md](coming-soon-campaign.md).

- **55–60s VO:** replace *"[NAME]. Free on the App Store. Stay Focused."* with → **"[NAME]. Coming late August. Follow so you don't miss it. Stay Focused."**
- **End-card on-screen text:** **"COMING LATE AUGUST 2026"** + **"follow so you don't miss it 🔔"** + logo + "Stay Focused."
- **Target date:** Fri **29 Aug 2026** (~80%, ±1 wk). Evergreen creative says **"late August"** (survives a ±1 wk slip); the exact **"29.08"** lives only in countdown stickers/pins. If it slips to September, swap "late August" → "September" — no other rework.
- **CTA mechanic:** *Follow for launch* (zero setup, grows the audience). Upgrade to **"Pre-order now"** once the App Store listing + final name are ready.
- **Hard rule:** `[NAME]` stays a placeholder — never print/lock the app name (not trademark-cleared). Hold final rendered audio + end-card until the **~15 Aug go/no-go** and the name lock.
- ⚠️ **Never announce** the ~4 Aug internal target — announce late August only.

## VO delivery direction

- **Voice:** young, warm, conversational — sounds like a mate showing you something cool, **not** an announcer and **not** hype-bro. Slight upward energy.
- **Pace:** brisk but not rushed; let the "watch this" (5s) and "rank up for showing up" (48s) lines breathe for half a beat.
- **Avoid:** any "transform your body / get shredded / summer body" energy — off-brand and teen-policy-risky.

## Production notes

- **First frame = the hook.** "stop screenshotting recipes" must be legible on mute in <0.5s.
- **Muted test:** watch it once with sound off — if the on-screen text alone doesn't sell it, tighten the text, not the VO.
- **Retention beats:** the cut at 12s (problem) and 20s (turn) are the two most likely drop-off points on a 60s TikTok — the visual *and* text both change hard there to re-hook.
- **All footage reusable:** every shot here already exists in the [video-concepts.md](video-concepts.md) batch-shoot list — no new captures needed beyond the "boring app" B-roll at 12–20s.
- **Cutdowns:** this 60s is the master. Pull the **0–20s** block as a standalone 15s ad, and the **42–50s** block as a standalone brand clip. One shoot → many assets.
- **Captions:** burn in captions for accessibility + the ~85–90% who watch muted.
- **Name:** don't render the logo/name into the final export until the name is locked + trademark-checked ([aso.md](aso.md)).
