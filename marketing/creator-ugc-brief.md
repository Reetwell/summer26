# BB — Creator / UGC Brief

> **Working name only** — the app name isn't final ([aso.md](aso.md)). Don't let creators burn a name into thumbnails/merch yet.
>
> **Role of this brief:** the primary content engine is faceless / screen-first (owned account). Creators are the **amplification + trust layer** — real young people showing the app on their own phones. This brief is what you send a creator.

---

## 1. The one-paragraph pitch (paste to creators)

> BB is a free fitness app for people your age — it builds your training plan, your meal plan, and your shopping list, and you can paste any TikTok/Reel and it turns into a saved recipe. It's *not* a weight-loss app: you rank up for **showing up**, not for what you weigh. Tagline: **Stay Focused**. We'd love you to show how you actually use it.

---

## 2. Who we want

- **Age 14–21** (UK + US). If under 18, we need a parent/guardian to approve and co-sign the agreement.
- **Micro / nano creators (2k–50k)** — higher trust + engagement + affordability than big names. Prioritise *fit* over follower count.
- **Niches:** everyday gym-goers, "student life / uni", meal-prep/"what I eat", "day in my life", productivity/"that girl/guy" routines, beginner fitness. **Not** shred/transformation/"summer body" accounts — wrong brand.
- **Vibe:** authentic, talks-to-camera naturally, non-preachy, body-positive.

---

## 3. What we want them to make

**Format:** 1 primary vertical video (15–30s), TikTok-native, screen-share of the app on their phone + their reaction/voiceover. Optional: 1 story/collab-post.

**Pick ONE angle (we'll assign based on their niche):**
1. **"Apps on my phone I actually use"** — BB as the fitness one; demo the TikTok→recipe trick.
2. **"How I meal prep as a student"** — meal plan → auto shopping list → Reminders.
3. **"A gym app that doesn't care what you weigh"** — the rank/league ethos.
4. **"POV: you don't know what to do at the gym"** — today-forward session + demos.
5. **Their own genuine take** — if they have a better idea that fits, we want it.

**Must include:**
- One real feature shown on screen (not just talking about it).
- The idea that it's **free** and **not a weight-loss app**.
- A clear on-screen mention of the app name + "link in bio / [store]".

---

## 4. Hooks that work (give them the menu)

- "Stop screenshotting recipes — do this instead."
- "POV: you never write a shopping list again."
- "A gym app that doesn't care what you weigh."
- "Walk into the gym knowing exactly what to do."
- "Free apps every [student / 16-year-old] should have."

First 2 seconds must land the hook on mute. (More in [video-concepts.md](video-concepts.md).)

---

## 5. Do / Don't (this protects everyone — read it)

**DO**
- Keep it **body-neutral and non-shaming** — focus on skill, consistency, food, "showing up."
- Be honest — show the real app; only demo the TikTok-import on a link you've tested (it can fail on some links).
- Disclose the ad clearly (see §6).
- Use your real voice; scripted-but-natural beats corporate.

**DON'T**
- ❌ No **weight-loss, calorie-deficit, "get shredded / summer body / fix your body"** framing. This is a hard line — it's off-brand *and* against UK ad rules for this audience (the ASA has repeatedly ruled such messaging irresponsible, and body/appearance-pressure content aimed at teens is restricted).
- ❌ No before/after, no weigh-ins, no "what I eat in a day to lose weight."
- ❌ No health/medical claims ("cures", "guaranteed results").
- ❌ No filming in a way that features other minors without consent.
- ❌ Don't promise features that don't exist — demo only what's on screen.

---

## 6. Disclosure & legal (mandatory)

- **UK (ASA/CAP):** any paid/gifted/incentivised post is an **ad** and must be **clearly labelled** — `#ad` prominent and up-front, **not** hidden behind "…more" or buried in hashtags. Applies even to gifted product/free access.
- **US (FTC):** clear + conspicuous `#ad` / "Paid partnership with [NAME]", in the video *and* caption.
- Use the platform's **paid-partnership / branded-content toggle** in addition to `#ad`.
- **Under-18 creators:** parent/guardian must review and co-sign; keep the agreement on file.
- **Content rights:** agreement must grant BB a licence to **repost/duet/boost** the content on owned channels and (for 18+ audiences) in whitelisted paid boosts.

## 8. Compensation approach (organic-first, cheap)

- **Wave 1 (seed):** gifted early access + a small flat fee for micro-creators; prioritise *fit*. Cheaper than any ad spend and more effective for minors (see [strategy.md](strategy.md) §4 — paid targeting to under-18s barely works, so this budget *is* the acquisition budget).
- **Wave 2 (amplify):** performance kickers (per-install or per-verified-signup) for the creators whose Wave-1 content performed.
- **Always:** repost/duet the best organic mentions for free — the flywheel is people posting *unpaid* because the TikTok→recipe trick is genuinely shareable.
