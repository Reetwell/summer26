# BB — 5 Vertical Video Ad Concepts (7–15s)

> **All concepts are faceless / screen-first** — buildable from screen recordings of the app + text-on-motion + trending audio. No talent, no founder on camera. A creator can *also* film any of these as a green-screen reaction (see [creator-ugc-brief.md](creator-ugc-brief.md)), but none require it.
>
> **Format spec:** 1080×1920, 9:16, safe-margin text (keep captions clear of the top ~250px and bottom ~350px where the TikTok UI sits). Brand: green `#00694c`, cream `#F9F6F0`, DM Serif Display for big words, DM Sans for the rest. End card on every one: logo + working name + "Free · [App Store / Play]".
>
> **Non-negotiable:** zero weight-loss / body-image framing. Every claim is a *demo of a real feature*, not a promise about bodies.

## Concept 1 — "Paste a TikTok, get the recipe" (THE hero)

**Feature:** Recipe import (TikTok/IG/YT link → editable recipe).
**Why it wins:** it's a party trick people *save and send to a mate*. Pure demo, no claim.

- **Length:** 12s
- **Hook (0–2s):** on-screen text over a paused cooking TikTok — **"stop screenshotting recipes."**
- **Beat-by-beat:**
  - 0–3s: a real food TikTok playing → finger taps *share → copy link*.
  - 3–7s: cut to BB → paste link → the recipe **builds itself** (ingredients + steps appear).
  - 7–10s: quick tap-edit one ingredient → tap **Save**.
  - 10–12s: it lands in "Saved recipes." End card.
- **On-screen text (sequence):** "stop screenshotting recipes" → "paste the link 🔗" → "…it just builds it" → "edit anything" → "saved forever. free."
- **Audio:** trending "satisfying / oddly satisfying" or a fast-cut transition sound.
- **CTA:** "the app that reads your TikToks → [NAME]".
- **Guardrail:** only ever film against a link that imports cleanly (see strategy §6).

## Concept 2 — "The shopping list writes itself"

**Feature:** Auto shopping list from the meal plan + Copy / Send-to-Reminders.
**Why it wins:** pure utility; students screenshot & share it; "send to Reminders" is a genuine wow.

- **Length:** 10s
- **Hook (0–2s):** **"POV: you never write a shopping list again."**
- **Beat-by-beat:**
  - 0–3s: meal plan week on screen, macros ticking.
  - 3–6s: tap → shopping list **auto-fills**, sorted by aisle (Produce, Dairy…).
  - 6–9s: tap **Send to Reminders** → cut to iOS Reminders with the list populated.
  - 9–10s: end card.
- **On-screen text:** "your meals →" → "…your shopping list, sorted" → "→ straight to Reminders" → "free."
- **Audio:** upbeat "life hack" audio or a trending productivity sound.
- **CTA:** "meal plan + shopping list, done for you."

## Concept 3 — "Rank up by showing up" (the differentiator)

**Feature:** Effort-based rank / weekly league.
**Why it wins:** this is what NO adult app does, and it's the non-shaming ethos on screen.

- **Length:** 13s
- **Hook (0–2s):** **"a gym app that doesn't care what you weigh."**
- **Beat-by-beat:**
  - 0–4s: Today screen → tap **mark session done** → streak ticks up, rank card animates.
  - 4–8s: cut to the **league / leaderboard** climbing (Bronze → Silver…).
  - 8–11s: text over it: "you rank up for *showing up*. not for shrinking."
  - 11–13s: end card.
- **On-screen text:** "doesn't care what you weigh 🚫⚖️" → "rank up for showing up" → "Bronze → Diamond" → "Stay Focused."
- **Audio:** a hyped/game-y "level up" trend audio.
- **CTA:** "effort-based. no body-shaming. free."
- **Note:** leans directly on the **"Stay Focused"** tagline — good brand-builder as well as an ad.

## Concept 4 — "The app just tells you what to do today"

**Feature:** Today-forward training (today's session hero, muscle map, animated demo, mark-done).
**Why it wins:** kills the #1 beginner blocker — "I don't know what to do at the gym."

- **Length:** 11s
- **Hook (0–2s):** **"walk into the gym knowing exactly what to do."**
- **Beat-by-beat:**
  - 0–3s: open app → today's session hero fills the screen.
  - 3–6s: tap an exercise → animated demo + **muscle map** lights up.
  - 6–9s: swap one exercise (tap → alternative) → mark set done.
  - 9–11s: week strip shows the plan ahead. End card.
- **On-screen text:** "no more standing around the gym" → "today's session, ready" → "how to do it + what it hits" → "swap anything" → "free plan."
- **Audio:** confident/gym trend audio (no aggressive hustle track — keep it welcoming).
- **CTA:** "personalised plan, free."

## Concept 5 — "Tired? It already adjusted."

**Feature:** Readiness / recovery check-in tuning today's training.
**Why it wins:** feels smart and *caring* (recovery-positive, anti-grind) — a fresh angle for the age group.

- **Length:** 9s
- **Hook (0–2s):** **"the app asked how I slept and changed my whole session."**
- **Beat-by-beat:**
  - 0–3s: 10-second readiness check-in (sleep / energy taps).
  - 3–6s: readiness score lands → today's session **auto-adjusts** (lighter/volume drop shown).
  - 6–9s: "rest is part of it." End card.
- **On-screen text:** "it asked how I slept 😴" → "readiness: low" → "…so it eased today off" → "recovery counts too."
- **Audio:** calm/soft trending audio (deliberate contrast to gym content).
- **CTA:** "trains smart, not just hard. free."

## Production notes (shared)

- **First frame = the hook.** Text must be legible on mute in the first 0.5s; ~90% watch on mute.
- **Show, don't tell.** These are demos; let the screen recording carry it. Minimal text.
- **Loopability:** end near where you began so the loop re-hooks (helps watch-time).
- **Batch-shoot:** record clean screen captures of each flow once (import, shopping, rank, today, readiness) → you can cut all 5 + dozens of variants from that footage.
- **Variant testing:** keep the same body, swap only the **hook line + audio** to find winners cheaply (see strategy §5, Phase 1).
- **Captions/name:** every end card marks the name as the working title until locked — don't burn the name into evergreen assets before [aso.md](aso.md) is finalised + trademark-checked.
