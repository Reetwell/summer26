Build Your Body · rank system · marketing brief

Ranked for showing up.

Bronze to Diamond — climbed on effort, streaks and consistency. Never on weight, size or strength. The one fitness rank that's fair for everyone.

Every tier is earned the same way — sessions done, streak days, goals hit. Everyone starts Bronze. Every week resets fresh.

The one rule: effort, not weight

Ranking invites comparison — so keep every cut, caption and thumbnail about turning up, not about bodies. This is the brand, and for a 14–21 audience it's also a policy line.

Do

  • Frame it as showing up, consistency, streaks.
  • Say it's anonymous + a fresh league weekly.
  • Treat Bronze as a proud start, never a failure.

Don't

  • No weight, size, calories, "shred", before/after.
  • No "beat your friends" — it's strangers at your pace.
  • No grind-till-you-drop; recovery is part of it.
