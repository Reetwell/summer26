## Delivery / voice settings (for ElevenLabs, CapCut, TikTok TTS, etc.)

- **Voice profile:** young adult (late teens/early 20s), warm, conversational — sounds like a friend showing you something cool. **Not** an announcer, **not** hype-bro.
- **Accent:** US for the US cut, UK for the UK cut (record both — you're launching UK+US).
- **Pace:** brisk but relaxed. Let two lines land with a beat after them: *"Watch this."* and *"…rank up for showing up."*
- **Emphasis:** hit *"free"*, *"showing up"*, and *"Stay Focused."*
- **ElevenLabs-style settings:** Stability ~40–50 (a little expressive), Similarity/Clarity ~75, Style exaggeration low-to-mid. Model: the multilingual/expressive v2-class voice.
- **Recommended ElevenLabs voices to try:** a bright young female (e.g. "Matilda"/"Jessica"-type) or a relaxed young male — audition 2–3, pick the one that sounds least "read-aloud."
- **Avoid:** any weight-loss/hype inflection — keep it friendly and non-shaming (matches brand + teen ad rules).
