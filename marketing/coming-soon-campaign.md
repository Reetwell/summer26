## Timeline (today = 10 Jul → 29 Aug)

### Phase A — Tease (14–31 Jul) · *build the account, no hard sell*
- **Goal:** warm up the TikTok/IG account + grow first followers before there's a date to push.
- **Content:** relatable/POV ("every gym app is boring, confusing, or wants your money…"), cryptic UI glimpses, "building something for people who train but hate spreadsheets." No app name (use the logo mark / "BB" only). Soft "something's coming."
- **CTA:** *Follow so you don't miss it.*
- **Cadence:** 4–5 posts/wk.

### Phase B — Reveal (1–14 Aug) · *show what it does + drop the date*
- **Goal:** reveal the product and announce "Coming late August."
- **Content:** the **60s hero ad** (coming-soon cut) as the centrepiece; the 5 feature teasers ([video-concepts.md](video-concepts.md)), lead with TikTok→recipe. First creator posts go live ([creator-ugc-brief.md](creator-ugc-brief.md)).
- **CTA:** *Coming late August — follow so you don't miss it.*
- **Cadence:** 1 post/day. **Checkpoint: 15 Aug go/no-go** — confirm 29 Aug before Phase C intensifies.

### Phase C — Countdown (15–27 Aug) · *daily, feature-a-day*
- **Goal:** convert followers into launch-day installs/opens.
- **Content:** near-daily countdown ("2 weeks", "10 days"…), one feature per day, best creator UGC, "save this for 29 Aug." Pin a countdown post; add a countdown sticker to Stories.
- **CTA:** *Follow + turn on notifications.* (Flip to "Pre-order now" if the App Store listing is ready.)
- **Cadence:** 1–2 posts/day.

### Phase D — Launch week (28 Aug–2 Sep) · *ramp + blast*
- **28 Aug:** "tomorrow." **29 Aug (launch):** "it's live" — flip every CTA to **"Download free — link in bio."** Repost every mention.
- **30 Aug–2 Sep:** momentum — first-user reactions, "here's what it does," ride any early UGC.
- **CTA:** *Download free on the App Store.*
