## Step 3 — Text style (match the brand)

- **Font:** DM Serif Display for the big line, DM Sans for the sub. (CapCut → Text → search "DM Serif Display" / "DM Sans"; if missing, use a close serif like "Playfair" + a clean sans.)
- **Colour:** text `#003F2E` deep green on light clips; if a clip is busy, put text on a cream `#F9F6F0` rounded bar at ~90% opacity.
- **Position:** top third, safe margins (keep clear of the top ~250px and bottom ~350px where TikTok's UI sits).
- **Animation:** "Fade" or "Rise" in, ~0.3s. Don't over-animate.
- **Emoji (optional):** 🟢🍝🛒🏋️🏆 — add to taste; they lift Gen-Z posts.

## Step 4 — Audio

1. **VO:** import the ElevenLabs render (US: `ad-teaser-features-vo-US-scratch.m4a` as a timing stand-in until you render the real one). Align lines to the marks above.
2. **Music:** add a *currently-trending* sound from CapCut/TikTok's library at ~15–20% volume under the VO. Trending audio changes weekly — pick it the day you post; it's the biggest reach lever.
3. **Duck** the music where the VO speaks (CapCut auto-ducking, or keyframe it down).

## Step 5 — Captions

- Auto-caption the VO (CapCut → Captions → Auto), then restyle to DM Sans, white with a thin dark outline. ~85–90% watch on mute.

## Step 6 — Export

- 1080×1920, 30fps, "Recommended" quality. Filename: `bb-teaser-v1.mp4`.
- Post-caption check: does it make sense on **mute** in the first 0.5s? If not, make the 0–3s text bigger.
