SummerBody — App Icon Concepts

Eight icon directions for the home-screen tile (wordmark stays in-app). All flat, no gradients or shadows, drawn only from your green ramp. Each shows a large tile plus a realistic ~52px home-screen version with label, so you can see how it actually reads on a phone.

01 · Letterform
Serif “S” — green tile
Off-white serif S on solid green. The classic, confident default. Pairs perfectly with the text wordmark.
